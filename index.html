About me

I am a final year Ph.D. student in the Electrical and Computer Engineering Department at UT Austin, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Sanjay Shakkottai.

My research interests lie in the fields of machine learning, optimization and networks. I am currently focused on developing and understanding decentralized machine learning algorithms. During my undergraduate degree, I have worked on performance analysis of wireless networks.
